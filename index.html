About Me

I am a Master's student at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and sensor fusion. My research focuses on robust perception for autonomous systems — particularly how radar and camera sensors can be combined to maintain reliable 3D object detection and tracking under adverse real-world conditions such as rain, fog, and low light.

I am passionate about building perception systems that work beyond the lab, bridging the gap between state-of-the-art research and robust deployment in the real world.

Projects

ICRA'26 Workshop

RadarMOT

Radar-Informed 3D Multi-Object Tracking under Adverse Conditions. Integrates radar as an explicit observation modality during tracking to recover missed detections, achieving 33.3% AMOTA vs. 22.8% CenterPoint baseline.

Radar-Camera Fusion

RaCaDet

Radar-Camera Fusion 3D Object Detection. Addresses camera depth ambiguity using radar as explicit spatial anchors and velocity priors, with a biased self-attention mechanism. Achieves 58.3% mAP and 64.0% NDS on nuScenes.

  • GitHub
Robotics · ROS 2

Autonomous Recycling Robot

Full-stack autonomous mobile robot developed in ROS 2. Features autonomous navigation with A* path planning, a 6-DOF arm for manipulation, object detection, and a behavior tree system for task orchestration.
