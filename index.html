Welcome to Bingxue Xu's Page 👋

I am a master's student in Autonomous Systems at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, supervised by Prof. Patric Jensfelt. My research focuses on robust 3D perception for autonomous driving and robotics, with a particular interest in multimodal sensor fusion.

Before that, I worked as a product engineer in the automotive R&D, covering product development and data management.

I am currently looking for full-time positions in autonomous driving or robotics after graduation. If my background seems relevant, please feel free to reach out.

Projects

RadarMOT

Radar-Informed 3D Multi-Object Tracking under Adverse Conditions

My master's thesis investigates leveraging radar information for robust 3D multi-object tracking when LiDAR or cameras fail, using a geometry-meets-learning approach.
